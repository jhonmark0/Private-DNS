Privacy Policy

Private DNS encrypts the DNS lookups your device makes. This page explains exactly what the app does with your data — the short version is nothing.

No accounts No analytics No tracking

What the app does

On Android, Private DNS runs a local VPN service that exists only to intercept DNS traffic on the device and send those lookups over an encrypted HTTPS connection (DNS over HTTPS) to the resolver. The native iPhone app uses an iOS Network Extension packet tunnel for DNS lookups and sends them to the same encrypted resolver. Only DNS lookups use the tunnel; other internet traffic is not routed through it. The iPhone setup website also provides a configuration profile as an alternative.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code that collects data. We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, or usage statistics.

DNS queries

Your DNS lookups are sent to the app’s encrypted resolver, which answers them and returns the result to your device. The app does not log, store, inspect or share those queries. The resolver processes them under its own privacy policy, which applies to the resolver, not to this app.

If you enter your own DNS server in the Android app’s settings, lookups go to that server instead, using plain DNS, and that server’s policy applies.

Stored on your device

  • Your custom DNS server addresses, if you enter any.
  • Whether protection was on or off the last time you used the app.

These settings stay on the device. They are not uploaded, synced, or shared anywhere.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data. There is no data to share: the app does not collect any.

The VPN connection

Android shows a VPN key icon while protection is on; iOS indicates its active VPN configuration in the system UI. Both tunnels are created on your device and only handle DNS traffic. They do not route, capture, or read your other network traffic. DNS lookups are sent encrypted to the resolver, whose privacy policy applies to those requests.

Children

The app is a network utility, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address listed on the app’s Google Play listing.
