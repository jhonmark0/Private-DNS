Every ad and tracker domain YouTube uses is blocked before it loads. Videos start straight away, with no pre-roll, no mid-roll and no banners — on your PC, your Android phone and your iPhone.

What it does

It sits between your device and the internet, and filters the requests ads rely on.

Blocks ads and trackers

Ad networks, analytics and tracking domains are refused, so pages load cleaner and load faster.

Encrypts every lookup

DNS is sent over HTTPS, so your internet provider and public Wi-Fi can’t see the sites you open.

One switch

Turn it on when you want it, off when you don’t. There is nothing to configure and no account to create.

Works everywhere

Wi-Fi and mobile data, in every app and browser. The iPhone profile needs no app at all.

Install

Windows

  1. Run Private DNS Setup and allow the permission prompt.
  2. Open the app and flip the switch on.
  3. Ads are blocked on every network.

Android

  1. Open the APK and allow installing from this source.
  2. Open Private DNS and flip the switch on.
  3. Allow the VPN request Android shows.

iPhone

  1. Tap Install on iPhone and allow the download in Safari.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded and install it.
  3. Select Private DNS under VPN & DNS.

Good to know

Everything is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen and Android may ask you to confirm before installing, and iOS labels the profile Not Verified. This is expected.

Turning the switch off, or removing the profile in Settings, restores your previous DNS settings.
